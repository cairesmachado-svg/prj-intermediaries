Dear Professor Levi-Faur,

I am writing in response to your call for a research assistant or research partner to help code intermediaries in EU legislation on the green transition.

By way of background, I am a postdoctoral researcher at ENAP (Brazil's National School of Public Administration), where I develop ARENAS-JUS, a research programme on judicial implementation arenas — the situated configuration in which policies, regulatory instruments, infrastructure, professionals, and users converge to produce concrete service conditions. The most advanced line of this programme is a documentary inventory of Conselho Nacional de Justiça (CNJ) acts on judicial infrastructure (2005–2025), built with a PRISMA-adapted protocol, a purpose-built codebook, rule-based evidence extraction, and a reproducible dataset — designed from the ground up to test how standards, monitoring, and governance instruments can be studied as components of regulatory capacity.

I found the framing of your call directly relevant to this work: identifying and coding intermediaries — the rule-brokers between regulators and regulatees — is methodologically close to what I have been doing with judicial governance instruments, only applied to a different legal corpus.
